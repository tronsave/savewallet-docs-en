# Privacy Policy

The Crypto Wallet App **"SaveWallet"** (hereinafter referred to as the **"App"**) is provided by **TronSave** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Company"**).

### 1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information that Users provide to the Company when using the App.

### 2. Information Collection

When Users use the App, the Company may collect the following personal information:

* Name and email address
* Information about the User's mobile device, including device ID, IP address, and operating system type
* The User's transaction history

{% hint style="info" %}
SaveWallet is **self‑custodial**. Your recovery phrase and private keys are generated and stored only on your device — the Company never receives, stores, or has access to them.
{% endhint %}

### 3. Purpose of Use

The Company will use the User's personal information for the following purposes:

* To provide App services
* To send notifications, announcements, or other content to the User
* For market research

### 4. Information Sharing

The Company will not share the User's personal information with third parties unless the User agrees or as required by law.

### 5. Information Security

The Company is committed to safeguarding the User's personal information using appropriate security measures, including:

* Data encryption
* Use of firewalls
* Access control

### 6. User Rights

Users have the following rights regarding their personal information:

* Request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information
* Request that the Company stop using their personal information

### 7. Contact

If Users have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Company:

* **Email:** [admin@tronsave.io](mailto:admin@tronsave.io)

The Company may update this Privacy Policy at any time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on the App or the Company's website. Users are responsible for reviewing the Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed.
