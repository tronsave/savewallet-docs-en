# Connect a dApp

## 🔗 Connect your Telegram Wallet to dApps with WalletConnect

The SaveWallet Telegram Wallet connects to decentralized apps (dApps) that support **WalletConnect**, so you can interact with them directly from your wallet. There are **two ways** to connect.

### Option 1 — Scan a QR code

**Step 1** — On the dApp (for example **Tronscan**), choose **WalletConnect** as the connection method.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/w0TrLasZyHO83dDQo4GF" alt="Choose WalletConnect on the dApp" width="420"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — A **QR code** appears on the dApp screen.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/sWZk36hfnKo382JTklXP" alt="WalletConnect QR code" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — In the Telegram Wallet, tap **Scan**, then **Scan QR Code**.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/ZAKcv2kT1CqL5Fv6Lyg5" alt="Scan button" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/73vQOIa4YMkMKBIupNhG" alt="Scan QR code" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 4** — Point your camera at the QR code, then **confirm** the connection in the wallet.

### Option 2 — Paste the connection URI

**Step 1** — On the dApp, choose **WalletConnect** and **copy** the connection URI.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/9BiiKh8fFsuD7E4jpD59" alt="Copy connection URI"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — In the Telegram Wallet, tap **Scan**, then **paste** the URI into the input field.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/TlAYjez0CflNoa4aDRBn" alt="Paste URI" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — Tap **Connect**, then **confirm** the connection in the wallet.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/rNq1W5vHmdJi4XYvw8jE" alt="Confirm connection" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Only connect to dApps you trust, and always review what each connection requests before you approve it.
{% endhint %}
