# How to send assets

**Step 1** — Open the SaveWallet WebApp and tap **Send**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/vOgQd3jgkFHXNmasSAgw" alt="Send button" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — Fill in the transfer form:

* **Receiving Account** — enter the address manually, scan a **QR code**, or tap **My Accounts** to choose from Recent, Address Book, or your own accounts.
* **Token** — tap the selector to choose the token to send.
* **Amount** — enter how much you want to send.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/iOxPwnR22cSweJotnJep" alt="Transfer form" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — Tap **Next**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/S3L3Yqy0YFtSxlrlw4jh" alt="Review" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4** — Review the details. For TRC‑20 tokens, Energy covers the fee — if you're short, SaveWallet **buys it automatically** at the best rate. You can uncheck **Buy Energy to consume** to opt out.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/NGWwk7OI5Zbo9J9ZpfuU" alt="Confirm with energy details" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5** — Tap **Confirm** to send the token.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/a6OwlFgC3goJ1VFmdQHG" alt="Transaction sent" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="success" %}
Automatic Energy renting from the Tronsave marketplace can save you up to **~80%** on network fees. [Learn more](../../mobile-app/savings-discover.md).
{% endhint %}
