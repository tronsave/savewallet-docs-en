# How to receive assets

**Step 1** — Open the SaveWallet WebApp and tap **Receive**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/1N3aiBqvSTaco1zpmKeN" alt="Receive button" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — Tap **Copy** to copy your address, or show the **QR code** for the sender to scan. Paste the address into the withdrawal page of an exchange or another wallet.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/5LkVPEpKamZ5nr8bFYvl" alt="Receiving address and QR" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
The same address receives TRX and every TRC‑20 token. If a token doesn't appear after a transfer, refresh and check that it's a standard TRC‑20 asset.
{% endhint %}
