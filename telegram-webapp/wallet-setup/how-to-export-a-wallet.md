# How to export a wallet

Back up your wallet by revealing its recovery phrase or private key. The WebApp asks for your passcode first.

## Export your recovery phrase

**Step 1** — Tap the **Settings** icon in the top‑right corner of the Home screen.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/h8alCe0Ite1insos3Av9" alt="Settings icon" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — Choose **Export Phrase**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/lPH1OZC7HgMtUB77pV5m" alt="Export phrase option" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — Enter your **passcode** to authenticate.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/Y8KuQk1x4m42cZ0rDIFR" alt="Enter passcode" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4** — Tap **Show Mnemonic Phrase** to reveal your recovery phrase, and store it offline.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/PwmxbjdEeTVBHVeV1zJ9" alt="Show mnemonic phrase" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Export a specific wallet's key

**Step 1** — Open the wallet list and tap the **More options** icon next to a wallet.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/r6wkwzJY9oYA67RgDupL" alt="Wallet list" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/kDLCrS5aZymJzYv7baXl" alt="More options" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2** — Choose **Export Wallet**, enter your passcode, and tap **Show Mnemonic Phrase** to view.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/BqK5qQTFEq5cip9o7hkK" alt="Export wallet" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/O3nE6w3LOkulMDre73FN" alt="Show phrase" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
Your private key and recovery phrase are not encrypted once revealed — keep them in a safe, offline place and never share them.
{% endhint %}
