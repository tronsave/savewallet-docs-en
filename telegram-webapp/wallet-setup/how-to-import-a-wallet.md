# How to import a wallet

Bring an existing TRON wallet into the SaveWallet Telegram WebApp using its recovery phrase or private key.

## Import as your first wallet

**Step 1** — Open the bot 👉 [SaveWallet — TRON Telegram Wallet](https://t.me/savewalletofficial_bot), tap **Start**, then **Open wallet**.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/1yucjYQRvtZiF2GF0kaD" alt="Start the bot" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/EPZ8ke5ARP6elD7TW6Gy" alt="Open wallet" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2** — Choose an import method: **Mnemonic Phrase** or **Private Key**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/q2cF9AlzLPNWHCbaKMDu" alt="Choose import method" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — Enter your mnemonic or private key and tap **Add Wallet**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/k4LlYYkZbr8UINWTUrLq" alt="Enter mnemonic or private key" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4** — Set a **passcode**, agree to the Terms of Service, and tap **Next**, then **Continue** to complete the import.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/UbhpXdpYlztWiLvyhe1S" alt="Set passcode" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Import into an existing setup

**Step 1** — Open the wallet list and tap **Add Wallet**.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/r6wkwzJY9oYA67RgDupL" alt="Wallet list" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/U0nDkB6I2wn3riHetaI0" alt="Add wallet" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2** — Choose **Import Wallet**, enter your passcode, fill in the mnemonic or private key, set a wallet name, then tap **Import Wallet**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/TxniEUkcW5HtdJKvI9cQ" alt="Import existing wallet" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Only enter your recovery phrase or private key inside the official SaveWallet WebApp. Never share it with anyone.
{% endhint %}
