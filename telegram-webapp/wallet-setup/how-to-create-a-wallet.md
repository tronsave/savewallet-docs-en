# How to create a wallet

Create a TRON wallet in seconds, right inside Telegram.

## Create a new wallet

**Step 1** — Open the official bot 👉 [SaveWallet — TRON Telegram Wallet](https://t.me/savewalletofficial_bot) and tap **Start**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/1yucjYQRvtZiF2GF0kaD" alt="Start the SaveWallet Telegram bot" width="280"><figcaption><p>Tap <strong>Start</strong> in the bot</p></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** — Tap **Open wallet** to launch the WebApp.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/EPZ8ke5ARP6elD7TW6Gy" alt="Open wallet" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3** — Tap **Create New Wallet**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/wJihMOWJWjzq9DtwOPNY" alt="Create New Wallet" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4** — Set a **passcode**, tick **I have read and agree to the Terms of Service**, then tap **Next**.

<figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/UbhpXdpYlztWiLvyhe1S" alt="Set passcode and agree to terms" width="280"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5** — Record your **Recovery Phrase** offline and verify it, then tap **Next** to finish. (You can tap **Skip** to create the wallet instantly and back up later — but back up before funding it.)

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/4o4Gjbmka6bWX9T49jCl" alt="Recovery phrase" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/yHSnJuEj897IHBcFXLNa" alt="Verify recovery phrase" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

## Generate a sub‑wallet

Need another wallet from the same setup?

**Step 1** — Open the wallet list and tap **Add Wallet**.

<div><figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/r6wkwzJY9oYA67RgDupL" alt="Wallet list" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://files.savewallet.io/U0nDkB6I2wn3riHetaI0" alt="Add wallet" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2** — Choose **Generate Sub Wallet**, enter your passcode, then tap **Add Wallet** to finish.
