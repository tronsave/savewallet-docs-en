# Transfer Tutorial

Moving assets in and out of SaveWallet is quick — and cheap, thanks to automatic Energy savings on TRC‑20 transfers.

* [How to send assets](how-to-transfer-assets-to-the-wallet.md) — send TRX or any TRC‑20 token.
* [How to receive assets](how-to-receive-my-assets.md) — share your address or QR code to get paid.

{% hint style="success" %}
When you send USDT or another TRC‑20 token, SaveWallet automatically rents cheap **Energy** from the Tronsave marketplace instead of burning TRX — saving you up to **~80%** on network fees. [Learn more](../savings-discover.md).
{% endhint %}
