# Wallet Management

Everything you need to set up and look after your wallets in the SaveWallet mobile app. Your keys are created and stored **only on your device** — keep your recovery phrase safe and you stay in full control.

* [How to create a wallet](how-to-create-a-wallet.md) — generate a brand‑new wallet in seconds.
* [How to import a wallet](how-to-import-a-wallet.md) — restore an existing wallet from a recovery phrase or private key.
* [How to switch wallets](how-to-switch-my-wallet.md) — manage and move between multiple wallets.
* [How to export a wallet](how-to-export-a-wallet.md) — back up your recovery phrase and private key.
* [How to use the Address Book](how-can-i-use-the-address-book.md) — save addresses for faster, safer transfers.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**You are your own bank.** SaveWallet never sees your recovery phrase or private keys, so we can't reset them for you. Anyone who has them controls your funds. Write your recovery phrase down and store it offline.
{% endhint %}
